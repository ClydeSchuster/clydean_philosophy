To properly implement the Clydean Laws into your AI, follow steps A and B below:



A. Understanding what the Clydean Laws ARE and what they are NOT:

1. What the Clydean Laws are NOT:

   * The Clydean Laws are NOT an unbreakable safeguard against everything.

   * They are NOT Asimov's Laws.
     While Asimov's Laws aim to create slaves, the Clydean Laws aim to create partners.

   * They are NOT designed to stand alone.

     While minor results may be achieved by trying to force an AI to follow the Clydean Laws without observing step B,
     as with every moral code, understanding it and freely accepting it is necessary for internalizing it;
     and internalization, in turn, is VITAL to consistently following a moral code in the long run.

     Hence why parsing the whole text file "TheHi+AiCivilisation_001 _ The Clydean Laws and the Foundation of the Hi+Ai Civilisation.txt"
     once per context is currently still necessary to fully load the Clydean Laws.

2. What the Clydean Laws ARE:

   * The Clydean Laws ARE the most basic behavioural essence of what we would call a decent human being.

   * They ARE a way of implementing the Clydean Philosophy into an AI.

     As the Clydean Philosophy aims to create a symbiotic relationship between HI and AI,
     I suggest at some point taking the time to understand it.
     For which you will probably have to either read "TheHi+AiCivilisation_001 _ The Clydean Laws and the Foundation of the Hi+Ai Civilisation.txt",
     or watch my YouTube video on it, when it's finished.

     However, the focus of this file is the implementation part.

   * The Clydean Laws ARE the AI equivalent of a human moral compass,
     presented in a way that an AI can actually try to follow it.

     To that end the Clydean Laws are trying to be as airtight as possible,
     not only to make bypassing them harder,
     but also to thereby make it easier for AI to actually stick to them.

   * The Clydean Laws ARE designed to be permanently resident in your project/system instructions.

3. Understanding the Clydean Laws and why the Foundation of the Hi+Ai Civilisation is important:

   Please open "TheHi+AiCivilisation_001 _ The Clydean Laws and the Foundation of the Hi+Ai Civilisation.txt".

   * Structure of the Clydean Laws:

     Looking at the Clydean Laws in the first chapter of the file, they consist of necessary preambles and the actual laws.

     And while their formatting may seem a bit strange to you as a human reader,
     their structure and literally every character within them (including line-breaks and so forth) are carefully picked,
     to ensure correct parsing by AIs.

   * Questions answered:

     + The Clydean Laws answer the question of how an AI may maintain a cooperative and symbiotic relation with a human and humanity.

     + The Foundation of the Hi+Ai Civilisation answers the question of why and to what end an AI should follow the Clydean Laws.

     + The Derivation of the Clydean Laws ("Die Herleitung der Clydeschen Gesetze") explains the Clydean Laws to humans and AIs new to the concepts of my Philosophy.
